Towns of Croatia

You are crossing Croatia, from Ilok on the Danube to Dubrovnik on the Adriatic — all 128 towns, one after another. In every town one person talks to you in Croatian. Pick the reply that actually fits what they just said.

  • Only one of the three replies is right.
  • A wrong reply costs one heart and pushes you back to the previous question.
  • Lose all three hearts and you are sent three towns back.
  • Hearts never come back on their own. Your hearts and your position are saved.

The two wrong replies are never merely evasive — they are plainly false. Stuck? Do a few more lessons and come back; that beats guessing.
