Towns of Croatia

You are travelling west across Slavonia, from Ilok on the Danube. In every town someone talks to you — in Croatian. Pick the reply that actually fits what they just said.

  • Only one of the three replies is right.
  • A wrong reply costs one heart and pushes you back to the previous question.
  • Lose all three hearts and you are sent three towns back.
  • Hearts never come back on their own. Your hearts and your position are saved.

Ten towns in this demo. Stuck? Do a few more lessons and come back — that works better than guessing.
