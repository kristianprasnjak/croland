## Tekst 1: Oglas
id: p14-01
naslov_en: Text 1: The advert
format: tekst
info: Marko dolazi raditi u Berlin i traži stan. Čita oglas.
info_en: Marko is coming to work in Berlin and looking for a flat. He reads an advert.
infokratko: Oglas za stan: *2-Zimmer-Wohnung, 3. Stock, kein Aufzug, 690 Euro kalt*.
infokratko_en: A flat advert: *2-Zimmer-Wohnung, 3. Stock, kein Aufzug, 690 Euro kalt*.
opis: Marko dolazi raditi u Berlin i čita oglas za stan. Samo čitaj i slušaj — ovdje se ne boduje.
opis_en: Marko is coming to work in Berlin and reads a flat advert. Just read and listen — nothing is scored here.
- hr: Marko traži stan u Berlinu. Savjet: oglas čitaj dvaput — *kalt* ovdje nema veze s vremenom.
- en: Marko is flat-hunting in Berlin. Tip: read the advert twice — here *kalt* has nothing to do with the weather.
- hr: Nepoznate riječi: *qm* (kvadratni metri), *NK* (Nebenkosten — režije), *ab sofort* (odmah), *der Altbau* (stara zgrada).
- en: Passive words: *qm* (square metres), *NK* (Nebenkosten — utilities), *ab sofort* (from now on), *der Altbau* (old building).
- 2-Zimmer-Wohnung in Neukölln, 52 qm, Altbau. | Dvosoban stan u Neuköllnu, 52 m², stara zgrada. // Two-room flat in Neukölln, 52 m², old building.
- 3. Stock, kein Aufzug. | 3. kat, bez dizala. // 3rd floor, no lift.
- Küche mit Herd und Kühlschrank, Bad mit Dusche. | Kuhinja sa štednjakom i hladnjakom, kupaonica s tušem. // Kitchen with cooker and fridge, bathroom with shower.
- Balkon zum Hof. | Balkon prema dvorištu. // Balcony facing the courtyard.
- Miete: 690 Euro kalt, 140 Euro NK. | Najam: 690 eura bez režija, 140 eura režije. // Rent: 690 euros cold, 140 euros utilities.
- Frei ab sofort. Keine Haustiere. | Slobodno odmah. Bez kućnih ljubimaca. // Available now. No pets.
- Besichtigung: Samstag, 11 Uhr. | Razgledavanje: subota, 11 sati. // Viewing: Saturday, 11 o'clock.
