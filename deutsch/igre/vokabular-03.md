## Dani i dijelovi dana
id: v03-01
naslov_en: Days and times of day
format: kartice
info: Dani i dijelovi dana su muškog roda — osim *die Nacht*. Prilozi *morgens* i *abends* pišu se malim slovom.
info_en: Days and times of day are masculine — except *die Nacht*. The adverbs *morgens* and *abends* are lower-case.
infokratko: Dani i dijelovi dana su **der** (*der Montag*, *der Abend*) — osim *die Nacht*. *morgens* = ujutro.
infokratko_en: Days and times of day are **der** (*der Montag*, *der Abend*) — except *die Nacht*. *morgens* = in the morning.
opis: Svi dani u tjednu su *der* — jedna stvar manje za pamćenje. Nema na čemu. Okreni karticu i ponovi naglas.
opis_en: Every day of the week is *der* — one less thing to remember. You're welcome. Flip the card and say it aloud.
- der Montag | ponedjeljak // Monday
- der Dienstag | utorak // Tuesday
- der Mittwoch | srijeda // Wednesday
- der Donnerstag | četvrtak // Thursday
- der Freitag | petak // Friday
- der Samstag | subota // Saturday
- der Sonntag | nedjelja // Sunday
- der Morgen | jutro // morning
- der Vormittag | prijepodne // late morning
- der Mittag | podne // noon
- der Nachmittag | poslijepodne // afternoon
- der Abend | večer // evening
- die Nacht | noć // night
- morgens | ujutro // in the morning
- abends | navečer // in the evening

## Koliko često? Kada?
id: v03-02
naslov_en: How often? When?
format: kartice
info: Riječi za učestalost i vrijeme. Obično stoje iza glagola ili na prvom mjestu.
info_en: Words for frequency and time. They usually go after the verb or in first position.
infokratko: Koliko često: *immer – oft – manchmal – selten – nie*. Kada: *heute*, *morgen*, *jetzt*.
infokratko_en: How often: *immer – oft – manchmal – selten – nie*. When: *heute*, *morgen*, *jetzt*.
opis: Okreni karticu, poslušaj i ponovi naglas. Ovim riječima Jonas opisuje svoj tjedan.
opis_en: Flip the card, listen and say it aloud. These are the words Jonas uses to describe his week.
- immer | uvijek // always
- oft | često // often
- manchmal | ponekad // sometimes
- selten | rijetko // rarely
- nie | nikad // never
- heute | danas // today
- morgen | sutra // tomorrow
- jetzt | sada // now
- dann | onda // then
- viel | mnogo // much, a lot
- zusammen | zajedno // together
- allein | sam, sama // alone
- noch | još // still; yet

## Mjesta i stvari tjedna
id: v03-03
naslov_en: Places and things of the week
format: kartice
info: Imenice sa članom. *das Wochenende* je srednjeg roda iako *die Woche* je ženskog.
info_en: Nouns with their article. *das Wochenende* is neuter even though *die Woche* is feminine.
infokratko: Imenice sa članom: *die Woche*, ali **das** *Wochenende*. Fraze: *zu Hause* = kod kuće.
infokratko_en: Nouns with their article: *die Woche*, but **das** *Wochenende*. Phrases: *zu Hause* = at home.
opis: Okreni karticu, poslušaj i ponovi naglas — imenice uvijek sa članom.
opis_en: Flip the card, listen and say it aloud — nouns always with their article.
- die Arbeit | posao, rad // work
- die Schule | škola // school
- der Kurs | tečaj // course
- die Woche | tjedan // week
- der Tag | dan // day
- das Wochenende | vikend // weekend
- die Universität | sveučilište // university
- das Café | kafić // café
- oder | ili // or
- denn | jer // because
- zu Hause | kod kuće // at home
- nach Hause | kući // home (direction)

- manchmal
- selten
- nie
