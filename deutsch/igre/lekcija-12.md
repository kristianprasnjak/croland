## Gdje je Ana?
id: l12-01
naslov_en: Where is Anna?
format: tekst
info: Ana obavlja poslove po Berlinu. Naučit ćeš dativ — padež za mjesto (*gdje?*) — i prijedloge *mit, bei, zu, von, aus, nach, seit*.
info_en: Anna runs errands around Berlin. You'll learn the dative — the case for place (*where?*) — and the prepositions *mit, bei, zu, von, aus, nach, seit*.
infokratko: Dativ za *gdje?*: *die Bank → in **der** Bank*, *der Park → **im** Park*.
infokratko_en: Dative for *where?*: *die Bank → in **der** Bank*, *der Park → **im** Park*.
opis: Ana obavlja poslove po Berlinu. Samo čitaj i slušaj — ovdje se ne boduje.
opis_en: Anna runs errands around Berlin. Just read and listen — nothing is scored here.
- Anna ist in der Bank. | Ana je u banci. // Anna is at the bank.
- Dann ist sie auf der Post. | Onda je na pošti. // Then she's at the post office.
- Jonas ist im Park. | Jonas je u parku. // Jonas is in the park.
- Sie warten am Bahnhof. | Čekaju na kolodvoru. // They wait at the station.
- hr: Ana danas juri po Berlinu: banka, pošta, park — a ti za njom. *Anna ist in der Bank.*
- en: Anna's dashing round Berlin today: bank, post office, park — and you're right behind her. *Anna ist in der Bank.*
- hr: Primijeti: *die Bank → in **der** Bank*, *der Park → **im** Park*. Član se promijenio — to je dativ, padež za *gdje?*.
- en: Notice: *die Bank → in **der** Bank*, *der Park → **im** Park*. The article changed — that's the dative, the case for *where?*.
- hr: Spoiler: *am Montag* i *mit dem Bus* koristiš od prvog dana. Danas napokon saznaješ zašto.
- en: Spoiler: you've been saying *am Montag* and *mit dem Bus* since day one. Today you finally find out why.

## Dativ: gdje?
id: l12-04
naslov_en: The dative: where?
format: tekst
info: Na pitanje *Wo?* (gdje?) iza *in, an, auf* ide **dativ**: *der → dem*, *die → der*, *das → dem*. Množina: *den* + *-n*.
info_en: For the question *Wo?* (where?), *in, an, auf* take the **dative**: *der → dem*, *die → der*, *das → dem*. Plural: *den* + *-n*.
infokratko: *Wo?* + *in, an, auf* → dativ: **dem** (m/n), **der** (ž), **den** (mn.). *in dem → **im** Park*.
infokratko_en: *Wo?* + *in, an, auf* → dative: **dem** (m/n), **der** (f), **den** (pl.). *in dem → **im** Park*.
opis: Pročitaj tablicu i poslušaj, pa u prazna polja upiši član u dativu.
opis_en: Read the table and listen, then type the dative article into the gaps.
most_hr: Kao hrvatski lokativ: *u parku, u banci, u kinu*. I u njemačkom se mijenja — samo što se promjena vidi na članu.
most_en: English just says "in the park". German changes the article: *in **dem** Park → **im** Park*.
- tab: | nominativ | dativ
- tab: muški // masc. | der Park | in dem Park → im Park
- tab: ženski // fem. | die Bank | in der Bank
- tab: srednji // neut. | das Kino | in dem Kino → im Kino
- tab: množina // plural | die Läden | in den Läden
- hr: *in dem → im*, *an dem → am*. Sad znaš zašto kažeš *am Montag* (razina 3)!
- en: *in dem → im*, *an dem → am*. Now you know why you say *am Montag* (level 3)!
- hr: **Sad ti.**
- en: **Your turn.**
- Anna ist in [der] Bank. | Ana je u banci. // Anna is at the bank.
- Max spielt [im] Park. | Max se igra u parku. // Max is playing in the park.
- Wir sind [im] Kino. | U kinu smo. // We're at the cinema.

## mit, bei, zu, von, aus, nach, seit
id: l12-06
naslov_en: mit, bei, zu, von, aus, nach, seit
format: tekst
info: Sedam prijedloga **uvijek** traži dativ: *mit* (s), *bei* (kod), *zu* (k, do), *von* (od), *aus* (iz), *nach* (u, nakon), *seit* (od, otkad).
info_en: Seven prepositions **always** take the dative: *mit* (with), *bei* (at), *zu* (to), *von* (from), *aus* (out of), *nach* (to, after), *seit* (since).
infokratko: Uvijek dativ: **mit, bei, zu, von, aus, nach, seit**. *mit **dem** Bus*, *bei **der** Oma*.
infokratko_en: Always dative: **mit, bei, zu, von, aus, nach, seit**. *mit **dem** Bus*, *bei **der** Oma*.
opis: Sedam prijedloga, jedan padež. Pročitaj tablicu i poslušaj, pa u prazna polja upiši član.
opis_en: Seven prepositions, one case. Read the table and listen, then type the article into the gaps.
most_hr: Kao hrvatski instrumental i genitiv: *s bratom, kod bake, iz kuće*. Njemački sve to rješava jednim padežom — dativom.
most_en: Memory trick: "aus-bei-mit-nach-seit-von-zu" — always dative.
- tab: prijedlog // preposition | primjer // example | znači // means
- tab: mit | mit dem Bus | autobusom // by bus
- tab: bei | bei der Oma | kod bake // at grandma's
- tab: zu | zum Bahnhof | do kolodvora // to the station
- tab: von | von der Arbeit | s posla // from work
- tab: aus | aus dem Haus | iz kuće // out of the house
- tab: nach | nach dem Essen | nakon jela // after the meal
- tab: seit | seit einem Jahr | već godinu dana // for a year
- hr: **Sad ti.**
- en: **Your turn.**

- Anna wohnt bei [der] Oma. | Ana stanuje kod bake. // Anna lives at grandma's.
