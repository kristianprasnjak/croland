info_en: Anna at passport control. The officer says *Sie* to her. Read line by line; the translation is right next to each sentence.

infokratko_en: Formal, with *Sie*: *Wie heißen Sie? Woher kommen Sie? Sprechen Sie Deutsch?*

opis_en: Just read and listen — nothing is scored here. Anna is at passport control.

- hr: Nepoznate riječi: *der Pass* (putovnica), *hier* (ovdje), *willkommen* (dobro došli).
- en: Passive words: *der Pass* (passport), *hier* (here), *willkommen* (welcome).

format: hoeren
info: Tri kratka predstavljanja, samo zvuk. Tko je odakle?

infokratko_en: Listen for *aus* (where from) and *in* (where): *Ich komme aus Kroatien. Ich wohne in Graz.*
