## gern — rado

info: *gern* iza glagola znači da nešto voliš raditi: *Ich schwimme gern* = volim plivati. Niječno: *nicht gern*.
info_en: *gern* after the verb means you like doing something: *Ich schwimme gern* = I like swimming. Negative: *nicht gern*.
infokratko: Što voliš raditi: glagol + ***gern***: *Ich schwimme gern.* Niječno *nicht gern*, a što više voliš: *lieber*.
infokratko_en: What you like doing: verb + ***gern***: *Ich schwimme gern.* Negative *nicht gern*; what you prefer: *lieber*.
opis: Što Jonas voli, a što Max ne voli? Pročitaj i poslušaj, a zatim u prazna polja upiši *gern*, *nicht* ili *lieber*.
opis_en: What does Jonas like, and what doesn't Max? Read and listen, then type *gern*, *nicht* or *lieber* in the gaps.
most_hr: Kao „rado plivam“. Njemački to kaže uvijek tako — nema posebnog glagola „voljeti raditi“.
most_en: German has no "like doing". You say "I swim gladly": *Ich schwimme gern.*
- tab: + | ++ | −
- tab: Ich lese gern. | Ich lese sehr gern. | Ich lese nicht gern.
- Jonas fährt gern Rad. | Jonas rado vozi bicikl. // Jonas likes cycling.

- hr: Što voliš više? *lieber*: *Ich lese lieber.* (Radije čitam.)
- en: What do you prefer? *lieber*: *Ich lese lieber.* (I'd rather read.)
- hr: **Sad ti.**
- en: **Your turn.**

- Anna kocht [nicht] gern. | Ana ne voli kuhati. // Anna doesn't like cooking.
