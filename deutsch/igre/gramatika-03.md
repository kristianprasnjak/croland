## Prezent u jednoj tablici
id: g03-01
naslov_en: The present tense in one table
format: tekst
info: Tri vrste osnova: obična (*mach-*), na *-t/-d* (*arbeit-*, dodaje *e*) i na *-s/-ß/-z* (*heiß-*, uz *du* samo *-t*).
info_en: Three kinds of stem: regular (*mach-*), ending in *-t/-d* (*arbeit-*, adds *e*) and ending in *-s/-ß/-z* (*heiß-*, only *-t* with *du*).
infokratko: Obična osnova (*du machst*), na -t/-d + **e** (*du arbeitest*), na -s/-ß/-z samo **-t** (*du tanzt*).
infokratko_en: Regular stem (*du machst*), -t/-d + **e** (*du arbeitest*), -s/-ß/-z just **-t** (*du tanzt*).
opis: Pročitaj i poslušaj tablicu. U prazna polja upiši glagol s pravim nastavkom.
opis_en: Read and listen to the table. Type the verb with the right ending into each gap.
- hr: Sve ovo si već radio, radila u lekciji — sad samo dobiva ime: prezent.
- en: You've already done all of this in the lesson — now it just gets a name: the present tense.
- tab: | machen | arbeiten | heißen | tanzen
- tab: ich | mache | arbeite | heiße | tanze
- tab: du | machst | arbeitest | heißt | tanzt
- tab: er / sie / es | macht | arbeitet | heißt | tanzt
- tab: wir | machen | arbeiten | heißen | tanzen
- tab: ihr | macht | arbeitet | heißt | tanzt
- tab: sie / Sie | machen | arbeiten | heißen | tanzen

- en: **Your turn.**

## Gdje je glagol?
id: g03-03
naslov_en: Where is the verb?
format: poredak
info: Poredaj dijelove rečenice. Glagol na drugo mjesto!
info_en: Put the parts of the sentence in order. The verb goes second!
infokratko: Vrijeme – **glagol** – subjekt – ostalo: *Am Wochenende spielt Max Fußball.*
infokratko_en: Time – **verb** – subject – rest: *Am Wochenende spielt Max Fußball.*
opis: Rečenica se raspala na dijelove. Poredaj ih — glagol na drugo mjesto!
opis_en: The sentence has fallen apart. Put the pieces in order — the verb goes second!
- Am Wochenende
- spielt
- Max
- Fußball.

## Glagol drugi — razvrstaj
id: g03-04
naslov_en: Verb second — sort them
format: razvrstavanje
info: Je li rečenica točna? Prvo mjesto može biti subjekt ili vrijeme, ali glagol mora biti drugi.
info_en: Is the sentence correct? The first position can be the subject or a time, but the verb must be second.
infokratko: Točno: *Heute kocht Jonas.* Netočno: *Heute Jonas kocht.* Glagol je **uvijek drugi**.
infokratko_en: Right: *Heute kocht Jonas.* Wrong: *Heute Jonas kocht.* The verb is **always second**.
opis: Točno ili netočno? Pronađi glagol i provjeri je li na drugom mjestu.
opis_en: Right or wrong? Find the verb and check whether it's in second place.
stupci: TOČNO // CORRECT | NETOČNO // WRONG
- Heute kocht Jonas. | TOČNO // CORRECT
- Jonas kocht heute. | TOČNO // CORRECT
- Am Montag arbeite ich. | TOČNO // CORRECT
- Abends lernt Anna Deutsch. | TOČNO // CORRECT
- Morgen spielen wir Tennis. | TOČNO // CORRECT
- Oft trinke ich Tee. | TOČNO // CORRECT
- Heute Jonas kocht. | NETOČNO // WRONG
- Am Montag ich arbeite. | NETOČNO // WRONG
- Abends Anna lernt Deutsch. | NETOČNO // WRONG
- Morgen wir spielen Tennis. | NETOČNO // WRONG
- Oft ich trinke Tee. | NETOČNO // WRONG
- Jonas heute kocht. | NETOČNO // WRONG

## Tri vrste pitanja
id: g03-05
naslov_en: Three kinds of questions
format: tekst
info: Pitanje da/ne: glagol prvi. W-pitanje: upitna riječ, pa glagol. Upitne riječi: *wer, was, wo, woher, wie, wann*.
info_en: Yes/no question: verb first. W-question: question word, then verb. Question words: *wer, was, wo, woher, wie, wann*.
infokratko: Da/ne: **glagol prvi** (*Kocht Jonas heute?*). W-pitanje: upitna riječ + glagol (*Wann kocht Jonas?*).
infokratko_en: Yes/no: **verb first** (*Kocht Jonas heute?*). W-question: question word + verb (*Wann kocht Jonas?*).
opis: Pročitaj i poslušaj. U prazna polja upiši upitnu riječ ili glagol.
opis_en: Read and listen. Type the question word or the verb into each gap.
- tab: vrsta // type | 1 | 2 | 3
- tab: izjava // statement | Jonas | kocht | heute.
- tab: da/ne // yes/no | Kocht | Jonas | heute?
- tab: W-pitanje // W-question | Wann | kocht | Jonas?
- hr: *wann* (kada) je nova upitna riječ. Odgovor je vrijeme: *Wann kochst du? — Um acht Uhr.*
- en: *wann* (when) is a new question word. The answer is a time: *Wann kochst du? — Um acht Uhr.*
- hr: **Sad ti.**
- en: **Your turn.**
- [Wann] arbeitest du? — Am Montag. | Kada radiš? — U ponedjeljak. // When do you work? — On Monday.
- [Was] machst du? — Ich koche. | Što radiš? — Kuham. // What are you doing? — I'm cooking.
- [Spielst] du Tennis? — Ja. | Igraš li tenis? — Da. // Do you play tennis? — Yes.

## Postavi pitanje
id: g03-06
naslov_en: Build the question
format: slaganje
info: Složi pitanje. Da/ne — glagol prvi; W-pitanje — upitna riječ pa glagol.
info_en: Build the question. Yes/no — verb first; W-question — question word, then verb.
infokratko: *Kochst du heute?* — glagol prvi. *Wo studiert Lena?* — upitna riječ, pa glagol.
infokratko_en: *Kochst du heute?* — verb first. *Wo studiert Lena?* — question word, then verb.
opis: Ana ima puno pitanja za cimera. Složi pitanje od riječi.
opis_en: Anna has lots of questions for her flatmate. Build each question from the words.
- Kochst du heute? | Kuhaš li danas? // Are you cooking today?
- Wann arbeitet Jonas? | Kada Jonas radi? // When does Jonas work?
- Was macht ihr am Sonntag? | Što radite u nedjelju? // What are you doing on Sunday?
- Hört Anna Musik? | Sluša li Ana glazbu? // Is Anna listening to music?
- Wo studiert Lena? | Gdje Lena studira? // Where does Lena study?
- Trinken Sie Kaffee? | Pijete li kavu? // Do you drink coffee?
- Wann spielen wir Tennis? | Kada igramo tenis? // When are we playing tennis?
- Wer kocht heute? | Tko danas kuha? // Who is cooking today?
- Brauchst du eine Lampe? | Trebaš li svjetiljku? // Do you need a lamp?
- Was sagt Herr Schulz? | Što kaže gospodin Schulz? // What does Mr Schulz say?

## Gdje stoji učestalost?
id: g03-07
naslov_en: Where do frequency words go?
format: izbor
info: *immer, oft, manchmal, nie* stoje iza glagola — ili na prvom mjestu, i onda subjekt ide iza glagola.
info_en: *immer, oft, manchmal, nie* go after the verb — or in first position, and then the subject goes after the verb.
infokratko: *oft, nie* iza glagola (*Ich trinke oft Kaffee*) — ili prvi, pa subjekt iza glagola (*Manchmal kocht Jonas*).
infokratko_en: *oft, nie* after the verb (*Ich trinke oft Kaffee*) — or first, then subject after verb (*Manchmal kocht Jonas*).
opis: Gdje stoji *oft* ili *nie*? Izaberi jedinu točnu rečenicu.
opis_en: Where does *oft* or *nie* go? Pick the one correct sentence.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Ich trinke oft Kaffee. | Ich oft trinke Kaffee. | Oft ich trinke Kaffee.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Manchmal kocht Jonas. | Manchmal Jonas kocht. | Jonas manchmal kocht.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Anna lernt immer abends. | Anna immer lernt abends. | Immer Anna lernt abends.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Wir spielen nie Fußball. | Wir nie spielen Fußball. | Nie wir spielen Fußball.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Selten hört Max Radio. | Selten Max hört Radio. | Max selten hört Radio.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Oft arbeitet Lena am Samstag. | Oft Lena arbeitet am Samstag. | Lena oft arbeitet am Samstag.

## Zamke
id: g03-08
naslov_en: Common traps
format: izbor
info: Tipične greške: engleski red riječi, pomoćni glagol kao u engleskom, krivi nastavak.
info_en: Typical mistakes: English word order, a helper verb as in English, the wrong ending.
infokratko: Bez pomoćnog glagola (*Kochst du heute?*), glagol drugi (*Heute spiele ich*), pravi nastavak (*Er arbeitet*).
infokratko_en: No helper verb (*Kochst du heute?*), verb second (*Heute spiele ich*), the right ending (*Er arbeitet*).
opis: Lov na greške: samo je jedna rečenica točna. Pronađi je.
opis_en: Spot the mistakes: only one sentence is right. Find it.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Kochst du heute? | Tust du heute kochen? | Du kochst heute?
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Er arbeitet viel. | Er arbeit viel. | Er arbeiten viel.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Heute spiele ich Tennis. | Heute ich spiele Tennis. | Heute spielen ich Tennis.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Ihr macht Musik. | Ihr machen Musik. | Ihr machst Musik.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Wann kommst du? | Wann du kommst? | Wann kommt du?
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Du heißt Max. | Du heißst Max. | Du heiße Max.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Wir warten hier. | Wir wartet hier. | Wir warten wir hier.
- Koja je rečenica točna? // Which sentence is correct? | Was sagt Anna? | Was Anna sagt? | Was sagen Anna?

## Napiši: moj tjedan
id: g03-09
naslov_en: Write: my week
format: schreiben
info: Opiši svoj tjedan u pet rečenica. Svaka počinje danom — pazi da glagol bude drugi.
info_en: Describe your week in five sentences. Each starts with a day — make sure the verb is second.
infokratko: Svaka rečenica počinje danom, glagol drugi: *Am Montag arbeite ich. Am Freitag koche ich.*
infokratko_en: Each sentence starts with a day, verb second: *Am Montag arbeite ich. Am Freitag koche ich.*
opis: Kako izgleda tvoj tjedan? Napiši pet rečenica i dobit ćeš povratnu informaciju.
opis_en: What does your week look like? Write five sentences and you'll get feedback.
zadatak: Napiši pet rečenica o svom tjednu. Svaka počinje danom (*Am Montag…*).
zadatak_en: Write five sentences about your week. Each starts with a day (*Am Montag…*).
uzorak: Am Montag arbeite ich. Am Dienstag lerne ich Deutsch. Am Mittwoch spiele ich Tennis. Am Freitag koche ich. Am Sonntag mache ich nichts.
rijeci: 25-50
- Pet rečenica, svaka počinje danom // Five sentences, each starting with a day
- Glagol je na drugom mjestu, subjekt iza njega // The verb is second, the subject after it
- Nastavak glagola odgovara osobi // The verb ending matches the person
- Dani pišu velikim slovom // Days are capitalised
