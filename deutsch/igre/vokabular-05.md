## Voće i povrće
id: v05-01
naslov_en: Fruit and vegetables
format: kartice
info: Voće i povrće s množinom. *das Obst* i *das Gemüse* nemaju množinu.
info_en: Fruit and vegetables with plurals. *das Obst* and *das Gemüse* have no plural.
infokratko: Voće i povrće s množinom: *die Tomate, Tomaten*. *das Obst* i *das Gemüse* nemaju množinu.
infokratko_en: Fruit and vegetables with plurals: *die Tomate, Tomaten*. *das Obst* and *das Gemüse* have no plural.
opis: Dobra vijest: *Banane, Tomate, Orange* — pola tržnice već znaš. Okreni karticu, poslušaj i ponovi riječ s množinom.
opis_en: Good news: *Banane, Tomate, Orange* — you already know half the market. Flip each card, listen and repeat the word with its plural.

- die Orange, Orangen | naranča // orange

- die Erdbeere, Erdbeeren | jagoda // strawberry
- die Karotte, Karotten | mrkva // carrot

- der Salat, Salate | salata // salad; lettuce

## Desetice
id: v05-07

format: memorija
info: Spoji broj i riječ.
info_en: Match the number and the word.
infokratko: Desetice na *-zig*: *vierzig, fünfzig, sechzig*. Iznimke: *dreißig* s *ß*, *sechzig* i *siebzig* kraći.

opis_en: Turn the cards two at a time and match each number with its word. The fewer moves, the better!

- 40 | vierzig
- 50 | fünfzig
- 60 | sechzig
- 70 | siebzig
- 80 | achtzig
- 90 | neunzig
- 100 | hundert
