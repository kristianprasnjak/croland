## Boje
id: v02-02
naslov_en: Colours
format: kartice
info: Boje su pridjevi: iza *ist* ostaju iste za sva tri roda. *orange* i *rosa* se nikad ne mijenjaju.
info_en: Colours are adjectives: after *ist* they stay the same for all three genders. *orange* and *rosa* never change.
infokratko: Boja iza *ist* se ne mijenja: *Der Tisch ist rot. Die Lampe ist rot.* *orange* i *rosa* nikad.
infokratko_en: A colour after *ist* never changes: *Der Tisch ist rot. Die Lampe ist rot.* *orange* and *rosa* never at all.
opis: Okreni karticu, poslušaj i ponovi naglas. Na buvljaku će ti trebati svaka boja.
opis_en: Flip the card, listen and say it aloud. At the flea market you'll need every colour.

- gelb | žut // yellow

## Male riječi
id: v02-03
naslov_en: Little words
format: kartice
info: Riječi koje opisu daju preciznost. *zu* ispred pridjeva znači „pre-“: *zu groß* = prevelik.
info_en: Words that make descriptions precise. *zu* before an adjective means "too": *zu groß* = too big.
infokratko: *hier* = ovdje, *dort* = tamo; **zu** ispred pridjeva = pre-: *zu groß* = prevelik.
infokratko_en: *hier* = here, *dort* = over there; **zu** before an adjective = too: *zu groß* = too big.
opis: Okreni karticu, poslušaj i ponovi naglas. S ovim riječima opis postaje precizniji.
opis_en: Flip the card, listen and say it aloud. These words make your descriptions sharper.
- hier | ovdje // here
- da | tu, ondje // there
- dort | tamo // over there
- was | što // what
- wirklich | stvarno // really
- ziemlich | prilično // quite, rather
- zu (zu groß) | pre- (prevelik) // too (too big)
- praktisch | praktičan // practical
