## Zapovijed: tri oblika
id: l09-06
naslov_en: Commands: three forms
format: tekst
info: Imperativ ima tri oblika: za *du*, za *ihr* i za *Sie*. Glagol ide na **prvo** mjesto.
info_en: The imperative has three forms: for *du*, for *ihr* and for *Sie*. The verb goes in **first** place.
infokratko: Tri oblika, glagol prvi: *Komm!* (du), *Kommt!* (ihr), *Kommen Sie!* (Sie).
infokratko_en: Three forms, verb first: *Komm!* (du), *Kommt!* (ihr), *Kommen Sie!* (Sie).
opis: Pročitaj tablicu i poslušaj. Zatim u prazna polja upiši zapovijed.
opis_en: Read the table and listen. Then type the command into the gaps.
most_hr: Kao *Dođi! Dođite!* — ali njemački ima i poseban uljudni oblik sa *Sie*: *Kommen Sie!*
most_en: English has one: "Come!" German has three, depending on who you speak to.
- tab: kome // to whom | kako // how | primjer // example
- tab: du | oblik za *du* bez *-st* i bez *du* // du-form without *-st* and without *du* | du kommst → Komm!
- tab: ihr | oblik za *ihr* bez *ihr* // ihr-form without *ihr* | ihr kommt → Kommt!
- tab: Sie | glagol + *Sie* // verb + *Sie* | Sie kommen → Kommen Sie!
- hr: **Sad ti.**
- en: **Your turn.**
- du trinkst → [Trink] Tee! | Pij čaj! // Drink tea!
- ihr bleibt → [Bleibt] hier! | Ostanite ovdje! // Stay here!
- Sie nehmen → [Nehmen] Sie die Tablette! | Uzmite tabletu! // Take the pill!

opis_en: A command doesn't have to sound like the army. Read, listen and type the missing word into the gaps.

most_en: *mal* is like "just": *Komm mal!* — "Just come here!"
