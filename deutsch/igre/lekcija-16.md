## Odjeća za razgovor
id: l16-01
naslov_en: The interview outfit
format: tekst
info: Lena ima razgovor za posao u novoj firmi. Što će odjenuti? Naučit ćeš **pridjev ispred imenice** — i njegove nastavke.
info_en: Lena has a job interview at a new company. What will she wear? You'll learn the **adjective before a noun** — and its endings.
infokratko: Iza *sein* bez nastavka: *Die Bluse ist **weiß***. Ispred imenice s nastavkom: *die weiß**e** Bluse*.
infokratko_en: After *sein*, no ending: *Die Bluse ist **weiß***. Before a noun, an ending: *die weiß**e** Bluse*.
opis: Lena sutra ima razgovor za posao, a Ana joj pomaže odabrati odjeću. Samo čitaj i slušaj — ovdje se ne boduje.
opis_en: Lena has a job interview tomorrow, and Anna is helping her choose an outfit. Just read and listen — nothing is scored here.
- Lena: Ich brauche eine schwarze Hose. | Lena: Trebam crne hlače. // Lena: I need black trousers.
- Anna: Und die weiße Bluse? Die ist schön. | Ana: A bijela bluza? Lijepa je. // Anna: And the white blouse? It's nice.
- Lena: Ja, und dazu ein blauer Blazer. | Lena: Da, i uz to plavi sako. // Lena: Yes, and a blue blazer with it.
- Anna: Perfekt! Du siehst super aus. | Ana: Savršeno! Izgledaš super. // Anna: Perfect! You look great.
- hr: Lena sutra ima razgovor za posao — a ormar je pun, ali „nema se što obući“. Poznato?
- en: Lena has a job interview tomorrow — the wardrobe is full, but there's "nothing to wear". Sound familiar?
- hr: Primijeti: *Die Bluse ist **weiß*** — iza *sein* pridjev nema nastavka (od razine 1). Ali *die weiß**e** Bluse* — ispred imenice ima!
- en: Notice: *Die Bluse ist **weiß*** — after *sein* the adjective has no ending (since level 1). But *die weiß**e** Bluse* — before a noun it does!
- hr: Hrvatski to radi stalno: *crn**i** kaput, crn**a** jakna*. Njemački također — samo po svojim pravilima. Do kraja razine i ti ćeš ih znati.
- en: English adjectives never change. German ones do — but by the end of this level you'll give them the right ending every time.

## Ispred imenice pridjev se mijenja
id: l16-02
naslov_en: Before the noun, the adjective changes
format: tekst
info: Kao u hrvatskom, pridjev ispred imenice dobiva nastavak. Dobra vijest: njemački ima samo **dva** nastavka za početak: **-e** i **-en** (+ *-er, -es* iza *ein*).
info_en: When an adjective comes before a noun, it takes an ending. Good news: German starts with just **two**: **-e** and **-en** (+ *-er, -es* after *ein*).
infokratko: Ispred imenice pridjev dobiva nastavak: *Der Mantel ist schwarz* → *der schwarz**e** Mantel*.
infokratko_en: Before a noun the adjective takes an ending: *Der Mantel ist schwarz* → *der schwarz**e** Mantel*.
opis: Isti pridjev, dva mjesta u rečenici. Pročitaj i poslušaj kako se mijenja — ovdje se ne boduje.
opis_en: Same adjective, two places in the sentence. Read and listen to how it changes — nothing is scored here.
most_hr: Hrvatski: *crn**i** kaput, crn**a** jakna, crn**o** odijelo*. Njemački radi slično — samo se nastavak dijeli između člana i pridjeva.
most_en: English adjectives never change: "a black coat, the black coat". German adjectives do — the ending depends on the article in front.
- Der Mantel ist schwarz. → der schwarze Mantel | Kaput je crn. → crni kaput // The coat is black. → the black coat
- Das Kleid ist rot. → das rote Kleid | Haljina je crvena. → crvena haljina // The dress is red. → the red dress
- Die Jacke ist neu. → die neue Jacke | Jakna je nova. → nova jakna // The jacket is new. → the new jacket

## Iza der: dovoljan je -e
id: l16-03
naslov_en: After der: -e is enough
format: tekst
info: Iza *der, die, das* član već pokazuje rod. Pridjevu je dosta **-e** — osim u muškom akuzativu i množini: **-en**.
info_en: After *der, die, das* the article already shows the gender. The adjective just needs **-e** — except masculine accusative and plural: **-en**.
infokratko: Iza *der/die/das*: **-e**. Muški akuzativ i množina: **-en** (*den rot**en** Pullover*).
infokratko_en: After *der/die/das*: **-e**. Masculine accusative and plural: **-en** (*den rot**en** Pullover*).
opis: Ana i Lena gledaju izlog. Pročitaj tablicu i poslušaj, pa u prazna polja upiši pridjev s nastavkom.
opis_en: Anna and Lena are looking at a shop window. Read the table and listen, then type the adjective with its ending into the gaps.
- tab: | muški // masc. | ženski // fem. | srednji // neut. | množina // plural
- tab: nominativ | der neu**e** Mantel | die neu**e** Jacke | das neu**e** Kleid | die neu**en** Schuhe
- tab: akuzativ | den neu**en** Mantel | die neu**e** Jacke | das neu**e** Kleid | die neu**en** Schuhe
- hr: Trik: kad se član promijeni u *den*, i pridjev dobiva *-en*. Množina uvijek *-en*.
- en: Trick: when the article changes to *den*, the adjective gets *-en* too. Plural is always *-en*.
- hr: **Sad ti.**
- en: **Your turn.**
- Der [rote] Pullover ist teuer. (rot) | Crveni pulover je skup. // The red jumper is expensive.
- Ich nehme den [roten] Pullover. (rot) | Uzimam crveni pulover. // I'll take the red jumper.
- Die [neuen] Schuhe sind bequem. (neu) | Nove cipele su udobne. // The new shoes are comfortable.

## Iza ein: pridjev pokazuje rod
id: l16-04
naslov_en: After ein: the adjective shows the gender
format: tekst
info: *ein* ne pokazuje je li riječ muška ili srednja. Zato to radi pridjev: **-er** (muški), **-es** (srednji).
info_en: *ein* doesn't show whether a noun is masculine or neuter. So the adjective does it: **-er** (masc.), **-es** (neut.).
infokratko: Iza *ein* pridjev pokazuje rod: *ein neu**er** Mantel*, *ein neu**es** Kleid*, *eine neu**e** Jacke*.
infokratko_en: After *ein* the adjective shows the gender: *ein neu**er** Mantel*, *ein neu**es** Kleid*, *eine neu**e** Jacke*.
opis: *ein* šuti o rodu — pa progovara pridjev. Pročitaj, poslušaj i u prazna polja upiši pridjev s nastavkom.
opis_en: *ein* keeps quiet about gender — so the adjective speaks up. Read, listen and type the adjective with its ending into the gaps.
- tab: | muški // masc. | ženski // fem. | srednji // neut.
- tab: nominativ | ein neu**er** Mantel | eine neu**e** Jacke | ein neu**es** Kleid
- tab: akuzativ | einen neu**en** Mantel | eine neu**e** Jacke | ein neu**es** Kleid
- hr: Vidiš uzorak? *der → -er*, *das → -es*, *die → -e*. Pridjev „posudi“ kraj člana kojeg nema.
- en: See the pattern? *der → -er*, *das → -es*, *die → -e*. The adjective "borrows" the ending of the missing article.
- hr: Isto vrijedi za *kein, mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser*: *mein neu**er** Mantel, keine neu**en** Schuhe*.
- en: The same goes for *kein, mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser*: *mein neu**er** Mantel, keine neu**en** Schuhe*.
- Das ist ein [schöner] Rock. (schön) | To je lijepa suknja. // That's a nice skirt.
- Ich suche ein [weißes] Hemd. (weiß) | Tražim bijelu košulju. // I'm looking for a white shirt.

## welcher i dieser
id: l16-07
naslov_en: welcher and dieser
format: tekst
info: *welcher?* (koji?) i *dieser* (ovaj) mijenjaju se kao *der*: *-er, -e, -es*, akuzativ muški *-en*.
info_en: *welcher?* (which?) and *dieser* (this) change like *der*: *-er, -e, -es*, masculine accusative *-en*.
infokratko: *welcher / dieser* kao *der*: *dies**er** Pullover, dies**e** Jacke, dies**es** Kleid*; akuzativ *dies**en***.
infokratko_en: *welcher / dieser* work like *der*: *dies**er** Pullover, dies**e** Jacke, dies**es** Kleid*; accusative *dies**en***.
opis: Koji? Ovaj! Pročitaj tablicu i primjere iz dućana i poslušaj ih — ovdje se ne boduje.
opis_en: Which one? This one! Read the table and the shop examples and listen — nothing is scored here.
- tab: | muški // masc. | ženski // fem. | srednji // neut. | množina // plural
- tab: nominativ | dies**er** | dies**e** | dies**es** | dies**e**
- tab: akuzativ | dies**en** | dies**e** | dies**es** | dies**e**
- Welcher Pullover gefällt dir? — Dieser hier. | Koji ti se pulover sviđa? — Ovaj ovdje. // Which jumper do you like? — This one here.
- Welche Jacke nimmst du? — Diese. | Koju jaknu uzimaš? — Ovu. // Which jacket are you taking? — This one.
- Welches Kleid kaufst du? — Dieses. | Koju haljinu kupuješ? — Ovu. // Which dress are you buying? — This one.
- hr: Iza *dieser / welcher* pridjev ima nastavak kao iza *der*: *dieser rot**e** Pullover*.
- en: After *dieser / welcher* the adjective takes the same ending as after *der*: *dieser rot**e** Pullover*.

infokratko_en: *Guten Morgen* = *(einen) gut**en** Morgen* — masculine accusative. *Gut**e** Nacht* — feminine.

- en: *Guten Morgen* is short for *Ich wünsche dir einen guten Morgen* — masculine accusative: **-en**!
- hr: Isto: *Guten Tag, Guten Abend, Guten Appetit, Schönen Tag noch!* Sve su muškog roda.
- en: The same goes for: *Guten Tag, Guten Abend, Guten Appetit, Schönen Tag noch!* All masculine.
- hr: A *Gute Nacht* i *Gute Reise*? *Die Nacht, die Reise* — ženski rod, pa **-e**.
- en: And *Gute Nacht* and *Gute Reise*? *Die Nacht, die Reise* — feminine, so **-e**.
