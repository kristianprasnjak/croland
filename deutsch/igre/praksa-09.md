## Tekst 1: Kod liječnice
id: p09-01
naslov_en: Text 1: At the doctor's
format: tekst
info: Ana je kod liječnice Weber. Prati upute u imperativu.
info_en: Anna is at Dr Weber's. Follow the instructions in the imperative.
infokratko: Upute sa *Sie*: *Machen Sie bitte den Mund **auf**.* *Atmen Sie tief.*
infokratko_en: Instructions with *Sie*: *Machen Sie bitte den Mund **auf**.* *Atmen Sie tief.*
opis: Ana je kod liječnice. Samo čitaj i slušaj — ovdje se ne boduje.
opis_en: Anna is at the doctor's. Just read and listen — nothing is scored here.
- hr: Liječnica Weber govori samo u zapovijedima — ali vrlo pristojno, uz *Sie* i *bitte*.
- en: Dr Weber speaks only in commands — but very politely, with *Sie* and *bitte*.
- hr: Nepoznate riječi: *tief* (duboko), *der Mund* (usta), *aufmachen* (otvoriti), *zweimal* (dva puta).
- en: Passive words: *tief* (deep), *der Mund* (mouth), *aufmachen* (to open), *zweimal* (twice).
- Guten Tag, Frau Horvat. Was fehlt Ihnen? | Dobar dan, gospođo Horvat. Što Vam je? // Hello, Ms Horvat. What's wrong?
- Ich habe Husten, und mir tut der Hals weh. | Imam kašalj i boli me grlo. // I have a cough and my throat hurts.
- Machen Sie bitte den Mund auf. | Otvorite usta, molim. // Please open your mouth.
- Atmen Sie tief. Noch einmal. | Duboko dišite. Još jednom. // Breathe deeply. Once more.
- Sie haben eine Erkältung. Nicht schlimm. | Imate prehladu. Nije strašno. // You have a cold. Nothing serious.
- Nehmen Sie die Tabletten hier zweimal am Tag. | Uzimajte ove tablete dva puta na dan. // Take these pills twice a day.
- Trinken Sie viel Tee und schlafen Sie viel. | Pijte puno čaja i puno spavajte. // Drink lots of tea and sleep a lot.
- Und bleiben Sie zwei Tage zu Hause! | I ostanite dva dana kod kuće! // And stay at home for two days!
