2. Who processes it

Supabase (authentication and database hosting) and Stripe (payment processing) act as our data processors. We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

3. Cookies / local storage

Croland stores your login session in your browser (via Supabase's standard session storage) so you don't have to log in every visit. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

4. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing kristian.prasnjak@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days, consistent with EU/GDPR data-portability and erasure rights.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and progress data for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile and progress data, except billing records Stripe must retain for legal/accounting purposes.

6. International transfers

Supabase and Stripe may process data outside your country; both provide standard contractual safeguards for such transfers.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
