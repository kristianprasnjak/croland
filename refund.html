Croland — last updated 5 October 2026

Purchases are not open yet. This policy applies from the day they open.

Who handles refunds

All purchases on Croland are sold by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record. Paddle handles payments, receipts and refunds. To ask for a refund, reply to your Paddle receipt email or go to paddle.net. You can also write to us and we'll help.

When you can get a refund

  • Within 14 days of a purchase you can request a refund. Paddle reviews each request under its refund policy and applicable consumer law.
  • After 14 days, payments are generally non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise.
  • Cancelling a plan stops future payments. It doesn't refund the current period, but you keep Plus until that period ends.

What happens after a refund

  • Plan: Plus from that payment ends.
  • Codes: codes from that purchase that haven't been redeemed yet stop working. Codes that were already redeemed stay valid for the people who redeemed them.

Charged by mistake?

If you don't recognise a charge from "PADDLE.NET* CROLAND", please contact us before contacting your bank — we'll sort it out quickly.
