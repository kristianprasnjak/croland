# Reading: Around Town
cjelina: Practice 13

## Text 1: My neighborhood
format: tekst
- Everyone is somewhere different, and it's Friday night.
- The locative on every corner — passive words: *sve tu* (everything's here), *najljepše* (the nicest), *u staroj zgradi / u maloj trgovini* (adjectives change in the locative too — later!), *poslijepodne* (in the afternoon), *u podne* (at noon), *sjede* (they sit).
- Moj kvart je mali, ali je sve tu. | My neighborhood is small, but everything's here.
- U centru je trg. | In the center there is a square.
- Na trgu je kafić. | On the square there is a café.
- U kafiću uvijek sjede ljudi. | People always sit in the café.
- Pošta je u staroj zgradi, a u pošti radi moja teta. | The post office is in an old building, and my aunt works at the post office.
- Škola je u parku, a u školi rade dobre učiteljice. | The school is in the park, and good teachers work at the school.
- Kupujem u maloj trgovini. | I shop in a small shop.
- Najljepše je na rijeci: na rijeci je uvijek mir. | The nicest place is by the river: by the river there is always peace.

## Text 2: Where are you?
format: tekst
- — Halo! Gdje si? | — Hello! Where are you?
- — U autobusu. A ti? | — On the bus. And you?
- — Ja sam još u uredu. Radim. | — I'm still at the office. I'm working.
- — U uredu?! Petak je navečer! | — At the office?! It's Friday evening!
- — Znam, znam... Gdje je Ana? | — I know, I know... Where is Ana?
- — Ana je u teretani. Trenira. | — Ana is at the gym. She's training.
- — A Marko? | — And Marko?
- — Marko je u kinu. Ili u kafiću. Ili u kinu, a poslije u kafiću! | — Marko is at the cinema. Or at the café. Or at the cinema, and afterwards at the café!
- — Dobro. Vidimo se u restoranu u osam! | — Fine. See you at the restaurant at eight!
- — Može! | — Sure!

## Who is where?

opis: Who is where? Match the person with the place.

- Ana | u teretani

## Text 3: A day in the city
format: tekst
- Subota je. | It is Saturday.
- Ujutro sam u pekari — kupujem kruh. | In the morning I'm at the bakery — I'm buying bread.
- Poslije sam na tržnici i kupujem voće. | Afterwards I'm at the market and I'm buying fruit.
- U podne sam u parku. | At noon I'm in the park.
- U parku čitam i gledam ljude. | In the park I read and watch people.
- Poslijepodne sam u muzeju. | In the afternoon I'm at the museum.
- U muzeju je tiho i hladno. | In the museum it is quiet and cold.
- Navečer sam u kazalištu. | In the evening I'm at the theatre.
- A poslije? Naravno — u kafiću! | And afterwards? Of course — at the café!
- U gradu nikad nije dosadno. | In the city it is never boring.

## Put the day in order
format: poredak
opis: Put the day in order — where is the narrator at each time?
- ujutro: u pekari
- poslije: na tržnici
- u podne: u parku
- poslijepodne: u muzeju
- navečer: u kazalištu
- na kraju: u kafiću

## True or false?
format: izbor
tekst: Ujutro sam u pekari — kupujem kruh. Poslijepodne sam u muzeju. U muzeju je tiho i hladno. Navečer sam u kazalištu. U gradu nikad nije dosadno.
opis: Tap true or false.
- Ujutro kupuje kruh u pekari. | TRUE | FALSE
- U muzeju je glasno. | FALSE | TRUE
- Navečer je u kinu. | FALSE | TRUE
- U gradu nikad nije dosadno. | TRUE | FALSE
