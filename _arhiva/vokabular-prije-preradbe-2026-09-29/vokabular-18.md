# Wishes & Dreams
cjelina: Vocabulary 18

## Dreams & helpers
format: kartice
opis: For dreaming out loud.
- bih, bi, bi, bismo, biste, bi | would (the conditional helpers)
- Htio bih... / Htjela bih... | I would like... (m./f.)
- san → snovi | dream → dreams
- sreća | happiness, luck
- zima | winter
- val → valovi | wave → waves
- zvijezda → zvijezde | star
- brod | boat
- ručak | lunch
