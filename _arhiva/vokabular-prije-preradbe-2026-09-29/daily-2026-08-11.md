# Grad u bojama 🎨
cjelina: Daily challenge 5

## Intro
format: tekst
- Remember the gender trick from the beach? 🌊 Today it pays off.
- Adjectives in Croatian *dress to match* their noun: **crven** auto, **crvena** kuća, **crveno** sunce. Same word, three outfits.
- The adjective copies the noun's ending: auto je **crven** (M — suglasnik) · kuća je **crvena** (Ž — -a) · sunce je **crveno** (S — -o).
