# Tko si ti? 👋
cjelina: Daily challenge 8
datum: 2026-08-14

## Intro
format: tekst
- The party from yesterday is still going — and people want to meet you. 🥳
- Four tiny word-pairs are all you need: **ja sam** (I am), **ti si** (you are), **on je** (he is), **ona je** (she is). Let's mingle.
- The verb changes with the person — that's why Croatians often drop the pronoun entirely: *Ja sam Ana. Ti si brat. On je konobar. Ona je sestra.*
