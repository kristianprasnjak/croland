# Reading: Our Club, My Hobby
cjelina: Practice 9

## Text 1: My hobby
format: tekst
- A club, a hobby, and a few people who take theirs seriously.
- Possessives in action — passive words: *ima / imaju* (has / have — properly in Lesson 10), *drugi* (a different one), *vani* (outside), *golovi* (goalposts), *Može!* (sure!), *to* (that).
- Moj hobi je fotografija. | My hobby is photography.
- Moj fotoaparat je star, ali je dobar. | My camera is old, but it is good.
- Moja sestra ima drugi hobi — njezin hobi je glazba. | My sister has a different hobby — her hobby is music.
- Njezina gitara je nova. | Her guitar is new.
- Naš brat voli sport. | Our brother loves sport.
- Njegova lopta je uvijek vani. | His ball is always outside.
- Naši hobiji su različiti, ali naša kuća je uvijek vesela! | Our hobbies are different, but our house is always cheerful!

## Text 2: Is this your ball?
format: tekst
- — Je li ovo tvoja lopta? | — Is this your ball?
- — Ne, moja lopta je crvena. To je njegova lopta. | — No, my ball is red. That is his ball.
- — Marko, je li ovo tvoja lopta? | — Marko, is this your ball?
- — Da! Hvala! Igramo li nogomet? | — Yes! Thanks! Are we playing football?
- — Može! Naš klub trenira danas, ali poslije smo slobodni. | — Sure! Our club trains today, but afterwards we are free.
- — Super. Vaš park ili naš park? | — Great. Your park or our park?
- — Naš — naš park ima nove golove! | — Ours — our park has new goalposts!

## Text 3: Our club
format: tekst
- Naš klub je mali, ali je dobar. | Our club is small, but it is good.
- Naš trener je strog, ali je pošten. | Our coach is strict, but he is fair.
- Moja pozicija je golman. | My position is goalkeeper.
- Ivan je kapetan — njegov dres je plav. | Ivan is the captain — his jersey is blue.
- Ana i Maja igraju odlično, njihova obrana je jaka. | Ana and Maja play excellently, their defence is strong.
- Subota je naš dan: igramo utakmicu! | Saturday is our day: we play a match!
- Naši roditelji gledaju i navijaju. | Our parents watch and cheer.

## Did you get it?
format: izbor

- Trener je strog, ali je... | pošten | veseo
- Moja pozicija je... | golman | kapetan
- Čiji dres je plav? | Ivanov | moj
- Tko navija? | roditelji | djeca
