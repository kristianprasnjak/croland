# Reading: A Day of Actions
cjelina: Practice 3

## Text 1: A day in the apartment
format: tekst
- A whole day of actions — see how much you catch on the first read.
- Verbs in the wild! Passive words: *čaj* (tea), *sir* (cheese), *strip* (comic book), *smiješan* (funny), *sjedi* (sits), *čeka* (waits), *kuha* (is cooking), *večer* (evening), *poslije* (afterwards), *kroz* (through), *Dobar tek!* (enjoy your meal), *svira* (is playing), the family — *tata* (dad), *mama* (mum), *brat* (brother), *sestra* (sister), all of them properly yours in Lesson 4 — plus *Što radiš?* (What are you doing?) and *Jedeš li?* (Are you eating?) — those last two are whole phrases for now, and Lesson 11 takes them apart.
- Marko radi. | Marko is working.
- On piše tekst. | He is writing a text.
- Ana uči. | Ana is studying.
- Ona čita udžbenik. | She is reading a textbook.
- Pas spava. | The dog is sleeping.
- Mačka gleda kroz prozor. | The cat is looking through the window.
- Marko pije sok. | Marko is drinking juice.
- Ana jede sendvič. | Ana is eating a sandwich.
- Radio svira. | The radio is playing.
- Dan je miran. | The day is calm.

## Text 2: What are you doing?
format: tekst
- — Bok, Ana! Što radiš? | — Hi, Ana! What are you doing?
- — Učim hrvatski. A ti? | — I'm learning Croatian. And you?
- — Ja gledam film. | — I'm watching a film.
- — Je li film dobar? | — Is the film good?
- — Da, jako je dobar. Što piješ? | — Yes, it's very good. What are you drinking?
- — Pijem čaj. Čaj je topao. | — I'm drinking tea. The tea is warm.
- — Ja pijem sok. Jedeš li? | — I'm drinking juice. Are you eating?
- — Da, jedem kruh i sir. | — Yes, I'm eating bread and cheese.
- — Dobar tek! | — Enjoy your meal!
- — Hvala! | — Thanks!

## Text 4: Sunday
format: tekst
- The longest text yet — and this time watch for one little word: **ne**. Passive words: *nedjelja* (Sunday), *nitko* (nobody), *danas* (today). Croatian likes its negatives doubled: *nitko **ne** radi* is literally "nobody isn't working", and that is exactly right.
- Nedjelja je. | It is Sunday.
- Danas nitko ne radi. | Today nobody is working.
- Ja ne učim i ne pišem. | I am not studying and I am not writing.
- Ja slušam radio. | I am listening to the radio.
- Marko ne spava. | Marko is not sleeping.
- On gleda film. | He is watching a film.
- Film je star i dobar. | The film is old and good.
- Ana ne čita udžbenik. | Ana is not reading a textbook.
- Ona čita pismo. | She is reading a letter.
- Pismo je lijepo. | The letter is beautiful.
- Pas ne jede sendvič. | The dog is not eating a sandwich.
- On gleda sendvič i čeka. | He is looking at the sandwich and waiting.
- Mačka ne sluša. | The cat is not listening.
- Ona spava. | She is sleeping.
- Nedjelja je lijepa. | Sunday is beautiful.
