# The Perfekt (Past Tense)
cjelina: Grammar 10

## The rule: the past tense formula
format: tekst
- One past tense covers everything in spoken Croatian: *I watched, I was watching, I have watched* — all simply **gledao sam**.
- **The formula:** biti (sam/si/je/smo/ste/su) + past participle. The participle: drop **-ti**, add the ending — he (or male ja/ti) → **-o** (gledao, pio, bio) · she (or female ja/ti) → **-la** (gledala, pila, bila) · they/we → **-li** (gledali, pili, bili) · neuter subject → **-lo** (bilo). Sound note: *jesti → jeo* (the d hides).
- **The speaker's gender matters:** a man says *Spavao sam dugo*; a woman says *Spavala sam dugo*. The verb itself tells the listener who's talking — English can't do that!
- **Word order — the leaning rule:** *sam/si/je...* never stands first: *Gledao **sam** film. · Ja **sam** gledao film. · Jučer **sam** gledao film.* You know this dance from the future (ću).
- **The past of "to be":** *Bio sam na moru. Voda je bila topla. Bilo je savršeno!*

## Type the participle
format: upis
opis: Type the participle.
- čitati (he) → | čitao
- kuhati (she) → | kuhala
- igrati (they) → | igrali
- biti (she) → | bila
- jesti (he) → | jeo

## The correct sentence
format: izbor
opis: Choose the correct sentence.
- I watched a film | Gledao sam film. | Sam gledao film.
- Ana sang | Ana je pjevala. | Ana je pjevao.
- we swam yesterday | Jučer smo plivali. | Jučer smo plivala.
- the pizza was excellent | Pizza je bila odlična. | Pizza je bio odličan.

## The rule: the past that didn't happen
format: tekst
- The participle doesn't move a letter. **Only the helper flips** — and you already know the flipped forms from Grammar 2.
- tab: Positive | Negative
- tab: Gledao **sam** film. | **Nisam** gledao film.
- tab: Ana **je** pjevala. | Ana **nije** pjevala.
- tab: Plivali **smo**. | **Nismo** plivali.
- tab: Bilo **je** savršeno. | **Nije** bilo savršeno.
- **And the word order relaxes.** *sam* had to lean on a first word; *nisam* is stressed and may open the sentence: ***Nisam** spavao dugo.* Both *Nisam spavao* and *Jučer nisam spavao* are fine.
- **Gender survives negation:** a man says *Nisam spavao*, a woman *Nisam spavala*, a group *Nismo spavali*.
- **The surprised question:** ***Nisi li** gledao utakmicu?* — "Didn't you watch the match?" Negative question, mild disbelief. Very Croatian.

## Say it didn't happen
format: upis
opis: Negate the past. The participle stays exactly as it is.
- Gledao sam film. → | Nisam gledao film
- Ana je pjevala. → | Ana nije pjevala
- Plivali smo jučer. → | Nismo plivali jučer
- Spavala sam dugo. → | Nisam spavala dugo
- Marko je radio. → | Marko nije radio
- Bili smo na moru. → | Nismo bili na moru

## The correct negative
format: izbor
opis: Choose the grammatical sentence.
- I didn't watch the film. (a man speaking) | Nisam gledao film. | Ne sam gledao film. | Nisam gledala film.
- She didn't sing. | Ana nije pjevala. | Ana ne je pjevala. | Ana nije pjevao.
- We didn't swim. | Nismo plivali. | Ne smo plivali. | Nismo plivala.
- It wasn't perfect. | Nije bilo savršeno. | Ne je bilo savršeno. | Nije bio savršeno.
- Didn't you watch the match? | Nisi li gledao utakmicu? | Li nisi gledao utakmicu? | Ne si li gledao utakmicu?

## Build the story
format: slaganje
opis: Build the story.
- Jučer je bila subota.
- Spavao sam dugo, a poslije sam pio kavu.
- Gledali smo film i jeli smo pizzu.
- Nisam radio jer je bila nedjelja.
- Nije bilo sunca, ali je bilo lijepo.
