## svoj or njegov?
format: izbor
opis: Does the thing belong to the person doing the action? Then it is *svoj*.

## Build the owner's word
format: upis
opis: A word in **-a** takes **-in**, any other takes **-ov**. Then the noun finishes the word.

- Ivan + gol → | Ivanov gol
