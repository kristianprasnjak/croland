# The Perfect Present: Asking Questions
cjelina: Lesson 11

## Time to ask
format: tekst
- So far you've mostly been *telling* — now you'll start *asking*. Today's mission: buy the perfect birthday present. To do that, you need questions: *What does she like? Who is it for? How much is it?*
- Seven little question words and one magic particle — **li** — and every conversation opens up.

## Question match
format: parovi
opis: Match each question with its logical answer. ("u Splitu" = in Split — ready-made phrase.)

- Što kupuješ? | Kupujem poklon.
- Gdje je Ana? | Ana je u Splitu.
- Kada je rođendan? | Sutra!
- Zašto voliš šah? | Jer je zanimljiv.
- Koliko godina imaš? | Dvadeset.

## Yes/no questions: the particle "li"
format: tekst
- No question word? Use **li** — glue it right after the verb:
- *Voliš čokoladu.* → ***Voliš li** čokoladu?* — Do you like chocolate? *Imaš psa.* → ***Imaš li** psa?*
- With *biti*, use the long forms: *Jesi li umoran? Je li to poklon?*
- And the friendly tag at the end: *Voliš glazbu, **zar ne**?* — You like music, *don't you*?

## Build the question
format: izbor
opis: Choose the correct question.
- Do you like fish? | Voliš li ribu? | Li voliš ribu?
- Do you have a brother? | Imaš li brata? | Imaš brata li?
- Is Ana at home? | Je li Ana doma? | Ana je li doma?
- Are you a student? | Jesi li student? | Si li student?
