format: slaganje
opis: The neighbours came in anyway. Give them the tour — one sentence at a time. ("vrata" is plural in Croatian, so it takes **su**.) Extra words are traps!

- Kuhinja je velika. | velika je | prozor
- Soba nije mala. | je | vrata
- Vrata su plava. | je | kuhinja
