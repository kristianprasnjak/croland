format: tekst
- You've been suspiciously agreeable for eleven lessons. Time to fix that.
- Today you learn to refuse soup, reject onions and declare that you *never* drink milk. Croatian negation is delightfully simple — one little word, **ne**, placed right before the verb. Plus two special verbs with attitude. Let's say no!

## Two exceptions: biti and imati
format: tekst
- Two verbs fuse with *ne* into one word:
- **biti:** nisam, nisi, nije, nismo, niste, nisu. **imati:** nemam, nemaš, nema, nemamo, nemate, nemaju.
- *Riba **nije** ukusna.* — Fish isn't tasty. ***Nemamo** ribu danas.* — We don't have fish today.

- Mi ___ vremena. | nemamo | ne imamo
- Oni ___ doma. | nisu | nemaju

## Double negatives are GOOD here
format: tekst
- In Croatian, negatives stack — and they *must*:
- *Marko **ništa ne** jede.* — Marko eats nothing. ***Nikad ne** pijem mlijeko.* — I never drink milk. *Ne jedem **ni** rajčicu **ni** luk.* — I eat neither tomato nor onion.
- English teachers hate this. Croatian grammar requires it. Enjoy the freedom.

## The picky eater
format: dijalog
opis: You're at a restaurant, but the kitchen is having a bad day. Navigate the menu!
- npc | Dobra večer! Izvolite?
- ti | Imate li ribu? | Imate li juhu?
- npc | Nažalost, danas nemamo ribu.
- ti | A juhu? | Što imate?
- npc | Imamo pizzu i salatu.
- ti | Ja ne jedem pizzu... onda salatu, molim. | Super! Pizzu, molim!
- npc | A za piće?
- ti | Samo vodu, molim. Ne pijem ništa drugo. | Imate li sok?
