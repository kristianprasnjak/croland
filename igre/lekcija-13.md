# Where Are You? The Locative
cjelina: Lesson 13

## Going vs. being
format: tekst
- Since Lesson 5 you can say where you're *going*: *Idem u grad.* Today you learn where you *are*: *Ja sam **u gradu**.*
- One tiny ending — **-u** for most words, **-i** for feminine ones — and the whole city becomes describable: at the café, in the park, at the office on a Friday night (hopefully not).

## The rule: being somewhere
format: tekst
- Compare: *Idem **u grad**.* — I'm going to the city (motion → accusative, from L5). *Ja sam **u gradu**.* — I am in the city (location → **locative**!).
- The rule for being somewhere: m. & n. → **-u** (u gradu, u kinu, u parku, u uredu); f. → **-a → -i** (u školi, na tržnici, u pekari).
- Same *u/na* logic as before: **u** = inside, **na** = on/at open places & events.
