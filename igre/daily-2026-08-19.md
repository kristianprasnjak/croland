## Intro
format: tekst
- Back at the market! 🧺 But today one apple won't cut it — you need two, three, five...
- Good news: Croatian plurals follow patterns you can *see*. Two letters do most of the work:
- ends in **-a**? Swap it for **-e**: banan**a** → banan**e**, jabuk**a** → jabuk**e**. Ends in a consonant? Add **-i**: telefon → telefon**i**, hotel → hotel**i**.
