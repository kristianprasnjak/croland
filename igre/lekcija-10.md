# Yesterday: The Past Tense
cjelina: Lesson 10

## Telling stories
format: tekst
- You can describe, ask, plan and dream in Croatian. One thing is missing: telling stories. *What did you do yesterday?*
- Good news: the past tense recycles something you've known since Lesson 2 — the verb *biti*. **Sam, si, je...** plus one new word form, and suddenly: *Gledao sam film. Bio je odličan!* — I watched a film. It was excellent!
- Let's talk about yesterday.

## The past participle
format: kartice
opis: Take a verb, drop -ti, add -o (he), -la (she), -li (we/they). Then add your old friend "biti" — Gledao sam film. Gledala sam film. Gledali smo film.
- gledati | gledao / gledala / gledali
- spavati | spavao / spavala / spavali
- piti | pio / pila / pili
- jesti | jeo / jela / jeli
- čitati | čitao / čitala / čitali
- biti | bio / bila / bili
- imati → imam | to have
- znati → znam | to know
- pobijediti | to win

## Who's talking?
format: tekst
- The participle shows the speaker's gender:
- *Plivao sam.* — said by a man. *Plivala sam.* — said by a woman. *Plivali smo.* — said by any group.

## Pick the right combo
format: izbor
opis: Choose the correct past form.
- Marko ___ tenis. | je igrao | je igrala | su igrali
- Ana ___ cijeli dan. | je učila | je učio | su učili
- Mi ___ pizzu. | smo jeli | sam jeo | je jela
- Voda ___ topla. | je bila | je bio | je bilo

## Word order
format: tekst
- Like the future helper, the past helper *never* goes first:
- *Spavao **sam** dugo.* / *Ja **sam** spavao dugo.* / *Jučer **sam** spavao dugo.*
- All three are fine — *sam* just always leans on the first word.

## Today → yesterday
format: upis
opis: It happened yesterday! Rewrite in the past — male or female form, both are accepted.
- Gledam film. → | Gledao sam film / Gledala sam film
- Pijem kavu. → | Pio sam kavu / Pila sam kavu
- Igramo nogomet. → | Igrali smo nogomet
- Ana uči. → | Ana je učila

## Build yesterday
format: slaganje
opis: Arrange the tiles.
- Jučer sam gledao film.
- Film je bio odličan.
- Pili smo kavu i pričali smo.
- Ana je pjevala, a Marko je svirao gitaru.
- Naš klub je pobijedio!

## How was it?
format: dijalog
opis: Your friend wants to know about yesterday. Choose your replies.
- npc | Bok! Gdje si bio jučer?
- ti | Bio sam na moru. | Bio sam u kinu.
- npc | Super! Što si radio?
- ti | Plivao sam i čitao sam knjigu. | Gledao sam film i jeo sam pizzu.
- npc | A je li bilo dobro?
- ti | Da, bilo je savršeno! | Film je bio odličan!
- npc | Sjajno! Sutra idemo zajedno!
