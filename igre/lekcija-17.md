# Take Command: The Imperative
cjelina: Lesson 17

## Time to give orders
format: tekst
- Time to give orders — politely, of course. Recipes, directions, a bossy football coach: they all run on the **imperative**.
- Croatian commands are short and punchy: *Uzmi!* (take!), *Idi!* (go!), *Miješaj!* (mix!). Today you'll learn them the best way there is: by making pancakes.

## Making commands
format: tekst
- Take the *ti* form, swap the ending: -am verbs → **-aj** (gledaš → gled**aj**!, dodaš → dod**aj**!); most others → **-i** (ideš → id**i**!, uzmeš → uzm**i**!, skreneš → skren**i**!); vowel verbs → **-j** (piješ → pi**j**!).
- For groups or politeness, just add **-te**: *idite, uzmite, pijte*. You've heard it for 16 lessons: *Izvoli**te**!*

## Saying "don't"
format: tekst
- Two flavors of don't:
- **Direct:** ne + imperative → *Ne trči! Ne gledaj telefon!*
- **Softer:** nemoj/nemojte + infinitive → *Nemoj trčati! Nemojte spavati!*
- Both are correct; *nemoj* sounds friendlier.

## Make it negative
format: upis
opis: Tell them NOT to do it — both ways work.
- Trči! → | Ne trči / Nemoj trčati
- Gledaj telefon! → | Ne gledaj telefon / Nemoj gledati telefon
- Spavajte! → | Ne spavajte / Nemojte spavati

## Lost in town
format: dijalog
opis: A tourist asks you for directions. Be the local hero!
- npc | Oprostite, gdje je kolodvor?
- ti | Idite ravno do semafora. | Nemojte ići ravno — skrenite desno!
- npc | Dobro. A onda?
- ti | Na semaforu skrenite lijevo. | Hodajte pokraj parka.
- npc | Hvala! Je li daleko?
- ti | Ne, ali požurite — vlak kreće za deset minuta! | Nije daleko. Nema žurbe!
- npc | Hvala vam puno!
- ti | Sretan put! I ne trčite preko ceste!
