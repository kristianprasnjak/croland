## Text 1: Dubrovnik
format: tekst
- Time to read real Croatian! These short texts use what you learned in Lesson 1 — plus a few passive words: *ali* (but), *ili* (or), *da* (yes), *vani* (outside), *jako* (very), *savršen* (perfect), *zid* (wall), *kafić* (café), *molim* (please), *hvala* (thanks), *izvolite* (here you are). Tap **EN** next to any sentence to see its translation.
- Dubrovnik je grad. | Dubrovnik is a city.
- Grad je star, ali je lijep. | The city is old, but it is beautiful.
- More je plavo i toplo. | The sea is blue and warm.
- Nebo je plavo. | The sky is blue.
- Hotel je velik i moderan. | The hotel is big and modern.
- Restoran je mali. | The restaurant is small.
- Kava je dobra. | The coffee is good.
- Sladoled je hladan i sladak. | The ice cream is cold and sweet.
- Dubrovnik je popularan. | Dubrovnik is popular.

## Text 2: The room
format: tekst
- Ovo je soba. | This is a room.
- Soba je mala, ali je lijepa. | The room is small, but it is beautiful.
- Stol je velik i star. | The table is big and old.
- Knjiga je nova. | The book is new.
- Lampa je žuta. | The lamp is yellow.
- Prozor je velik. | The window is big.
- Zid je bijel. | The wall is white.
- Krevet je mekan. | The bed is soft.
- Automobil je vani. | The car is outside.
- Automobil je crven i brz. | The car is red and fast.

## Text 3: At the café
format: tekst
- — Dobar dan! | — Good afternoon!
- — Dobar dan! Kava, molim. | — Good afternoon! A coffee, please.
- — Velika ili mala? | — Large or small?
- — Velika, molim. | — Large, please.
- — Izvolite. | — Here you are.
- — Hvala! Kava je topla i jako dobra. | — Thank you! The coffee is warm and very good.
- — Dan je lijep, sunce je toplo. | — The day is beautiful, the sun is warm.
- — Da, dan je savršen! | — Yes, the day is perfect!

## Order the dialogue
format: poredak
opis: The café conversation got shuffled! Put the lines back into the right order.
- — Dobar dan!
- — Dobar dan! Kava, molim.
- — Velika ili mala?
- — Velika, molim.
- — Izvolite.
- — Hvala! Kava je topla i jako dobra.
- — Dan je lijep, sunce je toplo.
- — Da, dan je savršen!
