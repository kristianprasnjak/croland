# Gdje je mačka? 🙈
cjelina: Daily challenge 35
datum: 2026-09-10

## Intro
format: tekst
- The cat is hiding. 🐱 Somewhere in this house, behind something, and she is *not* coming when called.
- To find her you need one tiny word: **u** = in. And here's the catch — the word after **u** changes its ending. *Kuhinja* → **u kuhinji**. *Soba* → **u sobi**. The word moved, so it changed shape.
- Feminine words swap **-a** for **-i**. Masculine and neuter words take **-u**: *vrt* → **u vrtu**, *auto* → **u autu**. That's the whole rule. Now go find her.

## The pattern
format: kartice
opis: Tap a card — same word, two shapes. Left is the thing, right is being *inside* it.
- kuhinja | u kuhinji — in the kitchen
- soba | u sobi — in the room
- kuća | u kući — in the house
- vrt | u vrtu — in the garden
- auto | u autu — in the car
- kino | u kinu — at the cinema

## Change the shape
format: parovi
opis: Match every place to the shape it takes after **u**. Six matches, one cat.
- kuhinja | u kuhinji
- soba | u sobi
- kuća | u kući
- vrt | u vrtu
- auto | u autu
- kino | u kinu

## The search
format: izbor
opis: Follow the clues. Where is she?
- (🍽 tanjur je prazan, mrvice na podu) Mačka je... | u kuhinji | u kuhinja | u kinu
- (🚗 vrata auta su otvorena) Mačka je... | u autu | u auto | u autom
- (🌳 lišće se miče) Mačka je... | u vrtu | u vrt | u vrta
- (🛏 nešto se miče pod dekom) Mačka je... | u sobi | u soba | u sobu
- "The cat is in the house." | Mačka je u kući. | Mačka je u kuća. | Mačka je u kuću.
