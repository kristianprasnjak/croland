## Intro
format: tekst
- Postcard from the Croatian coast! 🏖️
- Today's challenge: six beach words — and one tiny pattern that unlocks the gender of almost every Croatian noun. Spot it once, use it forever.
- Croatian nouns have a gender, and the ending usually gives it away: ends in **-a** → feminine (*plaža*), ends in a **consonant** → masculine (*brod*), ends in **-o / -e** → neuter (*more*).
