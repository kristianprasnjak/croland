# Presents & Questions
cjelina: Vocabulary 11

## Question words
format: kartice
opis: Everything you need to ask and wonder.
- tko? | who?
- što? | what?
- gdje? | where?
- kada? | when?
- zašto? | why?
- kako? | how?
- koliko? | how much / many?
- koji / koja / koje? | which?
- za koga? | for whom?
- li | yes/no question particle
- zar ne? | ...right? / isn't it?
