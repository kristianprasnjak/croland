# Volim / ne volim ❤️
cjelina: Daily challenge 27
datum: 2026-09-02

## Intro
format: tekst
- The most useful sentence in any language: *I like...* ❤️
- In Croatian it's one word — **volim**. And *I don't like*? Just add **ne**: **ne volim**. Today you sort your whole world into hearts and crosses.
- **Volim more.** — I like the sea. **Ne volim kišu.** — I don't like rain. *(spotted the -u? kiša → kišu)* **Volim kavu, ne volim čaj.** — both in one breath.
