# Negation
cjelina: Grammar 12

## The rule: ne + three fused verbs
format: tekst
- **The standard pattern:** ne + verb, always together, ne first: *Ne volim luk. Ne radim danas. Ne idemo u kino.*
- **The three fused verbs:** biti → **nisam, nisi, nije, nismo, niste, nisu** · imati → **nemam, nemaš, nema, nemamo, nemate, nemaju** · htjeti (futur) → **neću, nećeš, neće, nećemo, nećete, neće**. *Nisam umoran. Nemamo vremena. Neću plivati.*
- **Negation in the past:** the participle stays, only biti flips: *Gledao sam film.* → ***Nisam** gledao film.* · *Bila je sretna.* → ***Nije** bila sretna.*
- **Negatives must agree (double negation):** if one word is negative, they all go negative: *Ništa **ne** jedem. Nikad **ništa ne** kupujem!* (triple — perfectly correct!) *Ne pijem **ni** kavu **ni** čaj.* There is no "I eat nothing" with a positive verb — *ništa* demands *ne*.

## Make it negative
format: upis
opis: Negate it.
- Imam psa. → | Nemam psa
- Ona je doma. → | Ona nije doma
- Gledali smo utakmicu. → | Nismo gledali utakmicu
- Plivat ću. → | Neću plivati

## The rule: not this — but that
format: tekst
- *ali* joins two things that are **both true**. **nego** does something else entirely: it *corrects* a negative. It can only ever appear after a negation.
- tab: Croatian | English
- tab: Ne pijem kavu, **nego** čaj. | I don't drink coffee, but tea.
- tab: To nije čaj, **nego** kava. | That isn't tea, it's coffee.
- tab: Ne idem u kino, **nego** u kazalište. | I'm not going to the cinema, but to the theatre.
- **ali vs. nego, the one test:** *Kava nije dobra, **ali** je topla.* — both halves stand (a twist). *To nije kava, **nego** čaj.* — the first half is cancelled and replaced (a correction). English uses "but" for both; Croatian never does.
- **Comma obligatory** before *nego*, exactly as before *a* and *ali*.
- **The upgrade pattern:** *ne samo… nego i…* — *Ne govorim samo engleski, **nego i** hrvatski!* This is how you show off.
- **Careful:** *nego* is not a negation itself. The *ne* stays where it was — *Ne pijem kavu, nego čaj*, never *Pijem kavu, nego čaj*.

## ali or nego?
format: izbor
opis: Is the first half still true (ali), or is it being corrected (nego)?
- Kava nije dobra, ___ je topla. | ali | nego
- To nije čaj, ___ kava. | nego | ali
- Ne jedem ribu, ___ meso. | nego | ali
- Stan je mali, ___ je topao. | ali | nego
- Ne idem u kino, ___ u kazalište. | nego | ali
- Nisam umoran, ___ sam gladan. | nego | ali

## Correct it with nego
format: upis
opis: Cancel the wrong half and give the right one.
- Ne pijem kavu, ___ čaj. | nego
- To nije juha, ___ salata. | nego
- Ne volim luk, ___ volim rajčicu. | nego
- Ne govorim samo engleski, ___ i hrvatski. | nego
- Nije star, ___ nov. | nego

## Build the sentence
format: slaganje
opis: Build the sentence.
- Ne volim ni juhu ni ribu.
- Marko nikad ništa ne jede.
- Jučer nisam radio, ali danas radim.
- Ne pijem kavu, nego čaj.
- To nije problem, nego rješenje!
