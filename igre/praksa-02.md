## Text 1: Who is who
format: tekst
info: Your first connected reading. Every sentence uses the same frame — *ovo je* or a pronoun, then **biti**, then a job or an adjective — so read each line by asking who it is about: *On je kuhar*, *Ona je pametna i draga*.
- Time to read real Croatian about real people! These texts use Lesson 2 and Vocabulary 2 — plus a few passive words: *i* (and), *a* (and, whereas), *ali* (but), *također* (also), *kapetan* (captain), *trener* (coach), *vratarka* (goalkeeper, she), *odličan* (excellent), *život* (life), *Jesi li...?* (Are you...?), *Odakle si?* (Where are you from?), *molim* (please), *izvolite* (here you are).
- Tap **EN** next to any sentence to see its translation.
- Ja sam Ana. | I am Ana.
- Ja sam studentica. | I am a student.
- Ja sam mlada i visoka. | I am young and tall.
- Ovo je Marko. | This is Marko.
- On je kuhar. | He is a cook.
- On je jak i vrijedan. | He is strong and hard-working.
- Ovo je Petra. | This is Petra.
- Ona je doktorica. | She is a doctor.
- Ona je pametna i draga. | She is smart and kind.
- Ovo je Damir. | This is Damir.
- On je vozač, a Petra je doktorica. | He is a driver, and Petra is a doctor.
- On je zabavan, ali je i ozbiljan. | He is fun, but he is serious too.
- Mi smo prijatelji. | We are friends.
- Mi smo sretni. | We are happy.

## Did you get it?
format: izbor
info: A comprehension check on the text above; every answer is in it. The endings help you: a job in **-ica** and an adjective in **-a** point to a woman (*doktorica*, *draga*), while a bare consonant points to a man (*kuhar*, *vrijedan*).
tekst: Ja sam Ana. Ja sam studentica. Ja sam mlada i visoka. Ovo je Marko. On je kuhar. On je jak i vrijedan. Ovo je Petra. Ona je doktorica. Ona je pametna i draga. Ovo je Damir. On je vozač, a Petra je doktorica. On je zabavan, ali je i ozbiljan. Mi smo prijatelji. Mi smo sretni.
opis: Answer from the text.
- Who is the doctor? | Petra | Ana | Marko
- Who is the cook? | Marko | Damir | Petra
- Who is the driver? | Damir | Marko | Ana
- Ana is... | mlada i visoka | stara i niska | umorna
- Marko is... | jak i vrijedan | nizak i tužan
- Petra is... | pametna i draga | tužna i ljuta
- Damir is... | zabavan i ozbiljan | tužan
- They are... | prijatelji | turisti

## Text 2: Who are you?
format: tekst
info: A dialogue between two strangers who nonetheless use *ti*. Read for the two things they trade, job and origin, and notice *Ja sam Talijanka* against *Ja sam Hrvat*: the nationality word itself shows whether a woman or a man is speaking.
- — Bok! Ja sam Ivan. Tko si ti? | — Hi! I am Ivan. Who are you?
- — Bok! Ja sam Maja. | — Hi! I am Maja.
- — Jesi li ti studentica? | — Are you a student?
- — Ne, ja sam učiteljica. A ti? | — No, I am a teacher. And you?
- — Ja sam pilot. | — I am a pilot.
- — Pilot? To je super! | — A pilot? That's great!
- — Da, posao je težak, ali je zanimljiv. | — Yes, the job is hard, but it is interesting.
- — Odakle si? | — Where are you from?
- — Ja sam Hrvat, iz Splita. A ti? | — I am a Croat, from Split. And you?
- — Ja sam Talijanka, ali sam sada u Zagrebu. | — I am Italian, but I am in Zagreb now.
- — Drago mi je, Maja! | — Nice to meet you, Maja!
- — I meni, Ivane! | — Nice to meet you too, Ivan!

## True or false?
format: izbor
info: True or false on the dialogue above. Each statement is a *biti* sentence, so check the person and the ending: *Maja je Talijanka* is about her, *Ivan je Talijan* would be about him — one ending decides who a sentence is even describing.
tekst: — Bok! Ja sam Ivan. Tko si ti? — Bok! Ja sam Maja. — Jesi li ti studentica? — Ne, ja sam učiteljica. A ti? — Ja sam pilot. — Pilot? To je super! — Da, posao je težak, ali je zanimljiv. — Odakle si? — Ja sam Hrvat, iz Splita. A ti? — Ja sam Talijanka, ali sam sada u Zagrebu.
opis: Tap true or false.
- Maja je studentica. | FALSE | TRUE
- Maja je učiteljica. | TRUE | FALSE
- Ivan je pilot. | TRUE | FALSE
- Ivan je Talijan. | FALSE | TRUE
- Maja je Talijanka. | TRUE | FALSE
- Posao je težak. | TRUE | FALSE

## Order the dialogue
format: poredak
info: Reordering rather than translating: put the exchange back into sequence. Use the give and take of a conversation — a question is answered before the next one is asked, so *Tko si ti?* is followed by a name and *Odakle si?* by a place.
opis: The conversation got shuffled! Put the lines back into the right order.
- — Bok! Ja sam Ivan. Tko si ti?
- — Bok! Ja sam Maja.
- — Jesi li ti studentica?
- — Ne, ja sam učiteljica. A ti?
- — Ja sam pilot.
- — Pilot? To je super!
- — Odakle si?
- — Ja sam Hrvat, iz Splita.
- — Drago mi je, Maja!

## Text 3: At the café
format: tekst
info: The same patterns in the polite register: the waiter and the guests use *vi*, so the verb is *ste* and the adjective after it takes **-i** — *Vi ste ljubazni*. *Kako ste?* is simply the polite twin of *Kako si?*, and *Dobro sam* answers both.
- — Dobar dan! Kako ste? | — Good afternoon! How are you?
- — Dobro sam, hvala. A vi? | — I'm fine, thank you. And you?
- — I ja sam dobro. Vi ste turisti? | — I'm fine too. Are you tourists?
- — Da, mi smo turisti. | — Yes, we are tourists.
- — Super! Kava? | — Great! Coffee?
- — Da, molim. Ja sam jako žedan. | — Yes, please. I am very thirsty.
- — Izvolite. | — Here you are.
- — Hvala! Vi ste jako ljubazni. | — Thank you! You are very polite.
- — Nema na čemu. Doviđenja! | — You're welcome. Goodbye!

## Did you get it?
format: izbor
info: A comprehension check on the café dialogue. Let the verb tell you who is meant: *smo* means the guests are speaking as a group (*Mi smo turisti*), *sam* means one of them speaks alone (*Ja sam žedan*), and *ste* is the polite form aimed at them.
tekst: — Dobar dan! Kako ste? — Dobro sam, hvala. A vi? — I ja sam dobro. Vi ste turisti? — Da, mi smo turisti. — Super! Kava? — Da, molim. Ja sam jako žedan. — Izvolite. — Hvala! Vi ste jako ljubazni. — Nema na čemu. Doviđenja!
opis: Answer from the text.
- Who are they? | turisti | studenti | konobari
- How does the guest feel? | žedan | gladan | umoran
- The waiter is... | ljubazan | ljut
- What does "Nema na čemu." mean? | You're welcome. | See you!
- What does "Kako ste?" mean? | How are you? | Where are you from?

## Text 4: They are a team
format: tekst
info: A short description of a team, so plural subjects appear next to singular ones. Take one subject at a time: *Luka je brz*, *Ivana je hrabra*, and with a plural subject the adjective takes **-i** — *Vi ste odlični*, *Oni su sretni*.
- Ovo je tim. | This is a team.
- Luka je kapetan. | Luka is the captain.
- On je brz i jak. | He is fast and strong.
- Ivana je vratarka. | Ivana is the goalkeeper.
- Ona je visoka i hrabra. | She is tall and brave.
- Trener je star, ali je dobar. | The coach is old, but he is good.
- On je ozbiljan i vrijedan. | He is serious and hard-working.
- "Vi ste odlični!" | "You are excellent!"
- Oni su sretni. | They are happy.
- Sport je život! | Sport is life!
