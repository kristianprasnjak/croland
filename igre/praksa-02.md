## Text 1: Who is who
format: tekst
- Time to read real Croatian about real people! These texts use Lesson 2 and Vocabulary 2 — plus a few passive words: *i* (and), *a* (and, whereas), *ali* (but), *također* (also), *kapetan* (captain), *trener* (coach), *vratarka* (goalkeeper, she), *odličan* (excellent), *život* (life), *Jesi li...?* (Are you...?), *Odakle si?* (Where are you from?), *molim* (please), *izvolite* (here you are).
- Tap **EN** next to any sentence to see its translation.
- Ja sam Ana. | I am Ana.
- Ja sam studentica. | I am a student.
- Ja sam mlada i visoka. | I am young and tall.
- Ovo je Marko. | This is Marko.
- On je kuhar. | He is a cook.
- On je jak i vrijedan. | He is strong and hard-working.
- Ovo je Petra. | This is Petra.
- Ona je doktorica. | She is a doctor.
- Ona je pametna i draga. | She is smart and kind.
- Ovo je Damir. | This is Damir.
- On je vozač, a Petra je doktorica. | He is a driver, and Petra is a doctor.
- On je zabavan, ali je i ozbiljan. | He is fun, but he is serious too.
- Mi smo prijatelji. | We are friends.
- Mi smo sretni. | We are happy.

## Text 2: Who are you?
format: tekst
- — Bok! Ja sam Ivan. Tko si ti? | — Hi! I am Ivan. Who are you?
- — Bok! Ja sam Maja. | — Hi! I am Maja.
- — Jesi li ti studentica? | — Are you a student?
- — Ne, ja sam učiteljica. A ti? | — No, I am a teacher. And you?
- — Ja sam pilot. | — I am a pilot.
- — Pilot? To je super! | — A pilot? That's great!
- — Da, posao je težak, ali je zanimljiv. | — Yes, the job is hard, but it is interesting.
- — Odakle si? | — Where are you from?
- — Ja sam Hrvat, iz Splita. A ti? | — I am a Croat, from Split. And you?
- — Ja sam Talijanka, ali sam sada u Zagrebu. | — I am Italian, but I am in Zagreb now.
- — Drago mi je, Maja! | — Nice to meet you, Maja!
- — I meni, Ivane! | — Nice to meet you too, Ivan!

## Text 3: At the café
format: tekst
- — Dobar dan! Kako ste? | — Good afternoon! How are you?
- — Dobro sam, hvala. A vi? | — I'm fine, thank you. And you?
- — I ja sam dobro. Vi ste turisti? | — I'm fine too. Are you tourists?
- — Da, mi smo turisti. | — Yes, we are tourists.
- — Super! Kava? | — Great! Coffee?
- — Da, molim. Ja sam jako žedan. | — Yes, please. I am very thirsty.
- — Izvolite. | — Here you are.
- — Hvala! Vi ste jako ljubazni. | — Thank you! You are very polite.
- — Nema na čemu. Doviđenja! | — You're welcome. Goodbye!

## Did you get it?
format: izbor
tekst: — Dobar dan! Kako ste? — Dobro sam, hvala. A vi? — I ja sam dobro. Vi ste turisti? — Da, mi smo turisti. — Super! Kava? — Da, molim. Ja sam jako žedan. — Izvolite. — Hvala! Vi ste jako ljubazni. — Nema na čemu. Doviđenja!
opis: Answer from the text.
- Who are they? | turisti | studenti | konobari
- How does the guest feel? | žedan | gladan | umoran
- The waiter is... | ljubazan | ljut
- What does "Nema na čemu." mean? | You're welcome. | See you!
- What does "Kako ste?" mean? | How are you? | Where are you from?
