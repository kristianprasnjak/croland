# Što imaš u torbi? 🎒
cjelina: Daily challenge 37
datum: 2026-09-12

## Intro
format: tekst
- Airport security. 🛃 The officer points at your bag and says one word: *Otvorite.*
- You'll need the verb **imati** — to have. **Imam** = I have. **Imaš** = you have. **Ima** = he/she/it has. Notice the endings doing all the work: **-m** is me, **-š** is you, nothing is him or her.
- Open the bag.

## Words of the day
format: kartice
opis: Tap a card. Three forms, three things — everything you need for an inspection.
- imam | I have
- imaš | you have
- ima | he / she / it has
- torba | bag
- ključ | key
- novine | newspaper

## Fill the form
format: upis
opis: Type the missing form of **imati**. Look at who's doing the having!

- Ti ___ novine. | imaš
- Ona ___ torbu. | ima

## The inspection
format: dijalog
opis: Play the scene. Choose your replies — the officer is not in a joking mood.
- npc | Dobar dan. Otvorite torbu, molim.
- ti | Imam torbu. | Torba ima mene. | Ja sam torba.
- npc | Što je ovo?
- ti | To je ključ. | Ključ ima ključ. | Imaš ključ.
- npc | A ovo?
- ti | Imam novine i knjigu. | Novine imaju me. | Ja sam novine.
- npc | Imate li vodu?
- ti | Ne, nemam vodu. | Voda ima. | Imam more.
- npc | Sve je u redu. Ugodan put!
