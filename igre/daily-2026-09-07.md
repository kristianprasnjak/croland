## Intro
format: tekst
- One challenge, one whole week. 📅 Today you learn all seven days — and yes, **ponedjeljak** (Monday) is just as unpopular in Croatian.
- Bonus: Croatians don't capitalize day names. One rule less!

## Words of the day
format: kartice
opis: Tap a card — the mnemonics are built in!
- ponedjeljak | Monday ("po nedjelji" — after Sunday)
- utorak | Tuesday
- srijeda | Wednesday (sredina — the middle!)
- četvrtak | Thursday (četiri — 4th day!)
- petak | Friday (pet — 5th day!)
- subota | Saturday (≈ Sabbath)
- nedjelja | Sunday ("ne djelati" — no working 😴)

## Sort the week
format: slaganje
opis: The week fell apart! Tap the days back into order, Monday to Sunday.
