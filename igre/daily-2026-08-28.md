## Intro
format: tekst
- Tiny detail, big difference. 🔍
- On the shelf it's **kava**. But the moment you order it, drink it, or need it — it becomes **kavu**. Croatian words change shape when something *happens* to them.
- When a feminine word (-a) is the *target* of the action, **-a turns into -u**: Kav**a** je topla. → Pijem kav**u**. Other words don't change (for now — lucky you): *Pijem čaj. Gledam more.*
