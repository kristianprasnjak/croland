4. What other people can see

  • Friends you accept can see your display name and points, and more if you make your progress public.
  • If you redeem a code, the person who bought it can see your display name and points. You can hide this when redeeming or later in your settings, and you can choose to also share lessons and activity dates.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

5. Storage on your device

We keep your login session and a few preferences (language, interface layout, mini game state) in your browser's local storage. Before you have an account, Lesson 0 progress is kept for the browser session only. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

6. How long we keep data

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, progress, social connections and AI conversations. Codes you bought stay valid for whoever holds them, but are no longer linked to you. Paddle keeps billing records for as long as tax law requires.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data. On your Account page you can download all your data and delete your account yourself. You can also email us; we reply within 30 days. You can withdraw consent (for example to news emails) at any time. You have the right to complain to the Croatian data protection authority, AZOP, or the authority in your country.

8. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers (for example Google and Paddle) may process data outside the EU/EEA. They do so under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

9. Children

If you are under 16, a parent or guardian must agree to Croland's use of your data.

10. Changes

We'll update this page when something changes and tell you in the app or by email if the change is significant.
