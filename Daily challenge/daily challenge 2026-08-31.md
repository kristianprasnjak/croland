# Daily Challenge 2026-08-31 (Day 25) — Mi smo tim! ⚽

> **Interno:** Tip: gramatički mikro-koncept — množinske zamjenice *mi/vi/oni* + *smo/ste/su*. Direktan nastavak DC8 (ja/ti/on/ona) — ista mehanika parova, druga polovica tablice. Time je cijela paradigma glagola *biti* pokrivena kroz dva challengea.

**Tematska poveznica:** nogometna utakmica — tim, protivnici i navijači: mi, vi, oni.

---

## Screen 1 — Hook

Match day! ⚽ Your team warms up, the rivals arrive, the fans are loud.

You know *ja, ti, on, ona*. Today, the plural squad: **mi** (we), **vi** (you all), **oni** (they). Whole teams in one word.

---

## Screen 2 — The pattern

**Tip:** Card Showcase + mini-objašnjenje

Same trick as before — each pronoun has its verb partner:

- **mi smo** — we are → *Mi smo tim!*
- **vi ste** — you (all) are → *Vi ste dobri.*
- **oni su** — they are → *Oni su tu.*

**Kartice (audio):** mi smo · vi ste · oni su

## Screen 4 — Exercise 2: Stadium announcer

**Tip:** Sentence Builder
**Zadatak:** "You're on the mic — build each line for the crowd."

1. *We are the team!* → **Mi smo tim!** (ponuđeno: mi, smo, tim, su, ja) — *tim* kognat, dan s prijevodom
2. *They are good.* → **Oni su dobri.** (ponuđeno: oni, su, dobri, smo, dobar)
3. *You are loud!* → **Vi ste glasni!** (ponuđeno: vi, ste, glasni, si, oni) — *glasni* dano s prijevodom
4. *We are not small!* → **Mi nismo mali!** (ponuđeno: mi, nismo, smo, mali) — *nismo* pokazano u kartici prije zadatka (nije→nismo, ista logika kao DC10)

**Pravilo svladavanja:** sve rečenice točne.
