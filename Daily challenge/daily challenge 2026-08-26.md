## Screen 1 — Hook

Lazy Sunday. ☕ You read, you eat, you watch the rain — and today you say it all in Croatian.

The trick: Croatian "I"-verbs end in **-m**. *Čitam* = I read. One word — pronoun included, free of charge.

---

## Screen 2 — The pattern

**Tip:** Card Showcase + mini-objašnjenje

One word = a whole English sentence:

- **čitam** — I read / I'm reading
- **gledam** — I watch
- **pijem** — I drink

No "am", no "-ing", often no "ja" needed. The **-m** at the end means *me*.

**Kartice (audio + slika):** čitam · pišem · gledam · slušam · jedem · pijem

## Screen 4 — Exercise 2: Narrate your Sunday

**Tip:** Sentence Builder
**Zadatak:** "Build each sentence about your day."

1. *I'm drinking coffee... no wait — juice.* → **Pijem sok.** (ponuđeno: pijem, sok, jedem, čitam)
2. *I'm watching the sea.* → **Gledam more.** (ponuđeno: gledam, more, slušam, pišem)
3. *I read and I write.* → **Čitam i pišem.** (ponuđeno: čitam, i, pišem, jedem)
4. *I'm eating bread and I'm drinking tea.* → **Jedem kruh i pijem čaj.** (ponuđeno: jedem, kruh, i, pijem, čaj, gledam) — *čaj* dan s prijevodom kao mala najava sutrašnje teme

**Pravilo svladavanja:** sve rečenice točne; greška miješa riječi.
