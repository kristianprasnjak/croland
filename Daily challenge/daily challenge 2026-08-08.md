## Screen 2 — The pattern

**Tip:** Card Showcase + mini-objašnjenje

Croatian nouns have a gender, and the ending usually gives it away:

- ends in **-a** → feminine (*plaža*)
- ends in a **consonant** → masculine (*brod*)
- ends in **-o / -e** → neuter (*more*)

**Kartice s audiom i slikom:** more (sea), plaža (beach), brod (boat), riba (fish), sunce (sun), galeb (seagull)
