## Screen 3 — Exercise 1: Did you live it?

**Tip:** Multiple Choice (razumijevanje teksta)
**Zadatak (na ekranu):** "Five questions about your weekend — from memory!" *(gumb "Show story" postoji, resetira bodove runde)*

1. How does the hero feel on Friday? → tired / **happy** / angry
2. What do they buy at the concert? → a guitar / **a ticket** / juice
3. Where do they go on Saturday? → the market / the cinema / **the mountain**
4. Sunday morning = ? → football and beer / **coffee and a book** / tombola
5. What don't they like? → Croatian / Sunday / **Monday**

**Pravilo svladavanja:** 4/5 za prolaz; ispod toga tekst se ponovno nudi, pitanja se miješaju.
